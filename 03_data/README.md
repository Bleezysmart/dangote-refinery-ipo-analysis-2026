# Data

This folder contains the corrected analytical datasets used in the Dangote Refinery IPO project.

## Included datasets

- `financial_history.csv` — historical and H1 2026 financial performance and cash flow.
- `operations.csv` — capacity, utilization and gross refining margin.
- `product_sales.csv` — product-level revenue and available volume/realized-price data.
- `expansion_capex_schedule.csv` — remaining expansion spending schedule.
- `ipo_proceeds.csv` — gross proceeds, transaction costs and use of net proceeds.
- `ownership_dilution.csv` — pre-IPO ownership and post-IPO treatment.
- `refining_benchmarks.csv` — selected refinery and market benchmarks.
- `valuation_scenarios.csv` — Stress through Bull valuation scenarios.
- `risk_register.csv` — key investment risks and measurable drivers.
- `source_pack_csv/` — source map, collection priorities and data schema.

## Data-quality correction

The original product-sales workbook omitted three H1 2026 revenue lines: **CBFS, LPG and Propane**. These were added so the product-level revenue schedule reconciles to the reported H1 2026 revenue total of $13.91B.

A separate H1 2026 cash-flow reconciliation issue was retained as a documented limitation rather than silently replacing one reported figure with another.
