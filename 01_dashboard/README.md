# Power BI Dashboard

The final dashboard contains six pages covering the Dangote Refinery IPO analysis through H1 2026.

## Pages

1. **IPO At a Glance** — offer price, equity/business value, earnings turnaround, product revenue and operating momentum.
2. **Operating Performance** — revenue, EBITDA, margins, throughput and cash-flow trends.
3. **Peer Comparison** — utilization, market value and refining-margin benchmarks across selected global refiners.
4. **Growth & Funding** — expansion capital programme, IPO proceeds allocation and funding position.
5. **Valuation** — implied share prices, valuation multiples and the earnings required to support the IPO price.
6. **Investment Conclusion** — margin normalization, scenario range, risks and thesis-break conditions.

## Core valuation test

At ₦525/share, the model uses a post-IPO equity value of approximately $47.82B and business value of approximately $49.22B. Under the project's explicit 10× EV/EBITDA assumption, required sustainable operating earnings are approximately $4.92B versus an annualized H1 2026 run-rate of approximately $5.20B.

Annualized H1 2026 is a run-rate, not a forecast.
