# Research

This section documents the investment-research framework behind the Power BI dashboard.

## Research question

**Is ₦525 fair?**

The analysis separates reported facts, analyst calculations, explicit assumptions, scenario outputs and data limitations. It does not attempt to prove a predetermined investment conclusion.

## Main analytical areas

- IPO structure and offer terms
- Historical operating performance
- Revenue and product mix
- Profitability and cash flow
- Global refinery peer context
- Expansion programme and funding needs
- IPO proceeds and use of funds
- Valuation and earnings-power requirements
- Scenario analysis
- Risk and thesis-break analysis
- Methodology and limitations

## Valuation framework

The project's base valuation framework uses an explicit **10× EV/EBITDA** assumption. At the ₦525 offer price, required sustainable operating earnings are approximately **$4.92B**, compared with approximately **$5.20B** from annualizing H1 2026 operating earnings.

The scenario framework produces implied prices from **₦314 to ₦588** across Stress, Bear, Base, Strong and Bull cases.

## Important distinction

Annualized H1 2026 results are a run-rate, not a forecast. Refining-margin comparisons also require caution because peer companies do not all define or report refining margins in identical ways.
